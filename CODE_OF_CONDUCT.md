# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CorvusTUI is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people
can learn, ask questions, and contribute regardless of experience, background,
identity, or the model they happen to prefer.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Give actionable review feedback and explain the “why”.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a feature to protect scope, safety, or
  the release plan.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone's logs, keys, paths, or messages.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam, undisclosed promotion, or using issues for unrelated campaigns.
- Ignoring safety boundaries or pressuring maintainers to merge unsafe code.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat, and any
project space, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
privately through the address on the GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning,
a request to edit or remove content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent
ban. Decisions will be explained to the people involved when possible.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source codes of conduct, including the
Contributor Covenant. It is intentionally short so it can be read and applied.
